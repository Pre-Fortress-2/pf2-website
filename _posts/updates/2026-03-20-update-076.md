> IMPORTANT: This is not the Steam release. This is a patch to the Legacy version of Pre-Fortress 2

## Changes
---
- Updated various item pickups to be slightly cheaper to render
- Updated clipping, areaportals, textures on beams, and chimney models in Canalzone

## Bug fixes
---
- Fixed Napalm grenades playing a fizzle sprite at the end of it's lifespan instead of fading out the flame pool particle
- Fixed Napalm grenade flame pool bouncing on the ground when deployed on top of players models
- Fixed a bug where using the flag as civilian did not have a max flag speed
- Fixed a bug where every forced team change by maps failed to change the team of the first person textures
