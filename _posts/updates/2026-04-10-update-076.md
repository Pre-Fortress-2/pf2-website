> IMPORTANT: This is not the Steam release. This is a patch to the Legacy version of Pre-Fortress 2

## Changes
---
- Updated various item pickups to be slightly cheaper to render
- Updated clipping, areaportals, textures on beams, and chimney models in Canalzone
- Updated `item_generic` to play the sound of the highest priority resource that is restored
  - Health -> Armor -> Ammo -> Grenades

## Bug fixes
---
- Fixed the Napalm Grenade playing a fizzle sprite at the end of its lifespan instead of fading out with the flame pool particle
- Fixed the Napalm Grenade's flame pool bouncing on the ground when deployed on top of players models
- Fixed a bug where holding the flag as Civilian did not apply flag speed penalty where applicable
- Fixed a bug where forced team changes failed to update the viewmodel's team textures
- Fixed Sapper holstering animations erroneously playing the deploy animations
- Fixed a bug where Medic's Medigun speed buff negated flag speed penalty
- Fixed a bug where `pf_pickup_spawner` was unable to spawn backpacks
