# *Story time!* 
> If you're only here for Steam news scroll down to `SNEAK PEAK!`

We have been working on and off on some iteration of 0.8 ever since the release of 0.7, as it was always our plan for it to be the next big update. We ended up releasing a few small yet mighty updates which resulted in 0.8 continuing to morph into different forms and because of that, some aspects of 0.8 ended up in iterations of 0.7.

When the TF2 SDK was released in February of 2025 we were in the middle of developing 0.7.4 (The Cold Snap update) and we decided it was in the teams best interest to continue work on The Cold Snap update. While work continued on 0.7.4 we immediately began on setting up a Steam page and a working prototype of PF2 on the TF2 SDK or internally referred to as "Frog Build". We submitted our mod for review and waited **3 months** for Valve to approve our Steam page. In the meantime continued work on 0.7.4 while occasionally checking in our Store page status. Finally, in the beginning of May 2025, our Store page was approved for release. However, we chose to not release our Steam page right away as to avoid posting it at the same time as other mods, so that our mod could have it's own time in the spotlight. We decided that to avoid confusion between 0.7.4 and 0.8 (Steam Release) we would also wait to release the Cold Snap Update before our Steam release. So after 3 months of waiting for Valve to reply, and another 3 months of waiting for us to finish development, in August 2025, we finally released our Steam page to the world and we are so proud to have our years of hard work finally hit the Steam storefront.

Planning for version 0.8 started in September of 2025 as we took a month long break after releasing The Cold Snap update. During the planning stage we met weekly to put to pen and paper what our definitive content outline would be. No longer would ideas be loosely restricting to GitHub issues or developer design docs, this update would be planned out to it's entirety from start to finish. During this time our developers experimented on the TF2 SDK slowly working on porting our existing code as well as make concepts for new content. When October came around we put our focus towards reorganizing our development infrastructure by putting all of our outline into tangible GitHub issues that allowed our Developers to document their work and allow for the team to see what content had been started. November carried us into Early Development and our programmers began adding and removing SDK features to what was once a prototype and slowly transformed it into something that kind of resembled Pre-Fortress 2 as we knew it. December and January were slow months as the end of the year holidays and events took priority over our hobbies.

But February, that was the month of full on game development. We released 0.7.5 as a patch to our *now designated* Legacy version of Pre-Fortress 2 and released an updater/installer named Wings which took up a considerable amount of time in January. Wings is based on the installer we all know and love **BEANS!** *More specifically [`beans-rs`](https://github.com/ktwrd/beans-rs)*. We heard you PF Crew and we listened! We finally have an [open-source](https://github.com/Pre-Fortress-2/wings) universal platform updater and it *probably will only be used for 0.7.5*, but at least patches to our legacy version will be super easy!

We are excited to announce that Pre-Fortress 2 is **now accepting applications for volunteer developers** and those who are interested can view the **available roles in the [Join Us](/join-us) page**. We'll come back to this topic a little bit later but right now we have some previews we'd like to share with you!
